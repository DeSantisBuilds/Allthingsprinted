Which printing method?

The right method depends on your design, the material and how the piece will be used. Here’s a quick guide; we’ll recommend the best fit when you request a quote.

  • DTF printing

    Direct-to-film

    Your design is printed onto a film and heat-pressed onto the garment.

    Best for
    Detailed, multicolour graphics, gradients and full-colour logos.
    Typical uses
    T-shirts, hoodies, team and event apparel.

  • Heat transfer vinyl

    HTV

    Coloured vinyl is cut to shape and heat-pressed onto the garment.

    Best for
    Lettering, names, numbers and simple, solid-colour designs.
    Typical uses
    Team jerseys, personalized names, selected garment designs.

  • Embroidery

    Stitched thread

    Your logo is stitched into the fabric for a textured, premium finish.

    Best for
    A polished, professional look for logos and short text.
    Typical uses
    Polos, hats, uniforms and corporate clothing.

  • Vinyl graphics

    Adhesive vinyl

    Lettering and graphics are cut from adhesive vinyl and applied to a surface.

    Best for
    Clean, sharp lettering and logos on hard, smooth surfaces.
    Typical uses
    Windows, storefronts, walls, vehicles and signs.

Not sure which one you need? Describe your project and we’ll recommend the right material and method.

Frequently asked questions

How long does an order take?

Typical turnaround is 10 to 15 business days. If you have a deadline, include it in your quote request.

What file format should I send?

Vector files (such as AI, EPS, SVG or a vector PDF) are print-ready. If your artwork isn’t vectorized (for example a JPG or PNG), we can adjust it for you; a fee applies for the adjustment.

Will I see my design before it’s printed?

Yes. Visual mockups are included in our pricing and are sent once your quote is approved.

How does payment work?

We ask for a 50% deposit to confirm your order, and the remaining 50% on completion.

Do you deliver?

Yes. We serve Greater Montreal. Delivery fees may apply depending on your order and location.

Which printing method is right for my project?

It depends on your design, garment or surface. See our printing method guide, or describe your project and we’ll recommend the best option.

I don’t have a design yet. Can you help?

Yes. Our design and artwork preparation service covers logo preparation, cleanup, vectorization, layout and production mockups.
