FAQ

How long does it take?

Typical turnaround is 10 to 15 business days.

What file should I send?

A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or PDF) is best. Otherwise, we can adjust your file for a fee.

Will I see my design before printing?

Yes. The visual mockup is included in the price and sent once your quote is approved.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit to confirm the order, and the remaining 50% on completion.

Do you deliver?

Yes, in Greater Montreal. Delivery fees may apply.

Which method should I choose?

DTF for colourful, detailed designs, heat transfer vinyl for names and numbers, embroidery for a premium look, and adhesive vinyl for windows and vehicles. We’ll advise you.
