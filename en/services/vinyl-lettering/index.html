Frequently asked questions

What file should I send?

A vector file (AI, EPS, SVG or vector PDF) is best. If your file isn’t vectorized, we can adjust it; a fee applies for the adjustment.

Will I see my design before printing?

Yes. The visual mockup is included in the price and sent to you once your quote is approved.

How long does an order take?

Typical turnaround is 10 to 15 business days. If you have a deadline, include it in your request.

How does payment work?

A 50% deposit confirms the order, and the remaining 50% is due on completion.
