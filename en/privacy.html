Privacy policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

All Things Printed (“we”) respects the privacy of its customers and website visitors. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why, and how we protect it, in accordance with Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector.

Person in charge of the protection of personal information

For any question or request about your personal information, contact the person in charge of the protection of personal information at All Things Printed:
Email: info@allthingsprinted.ca
Phone: (514) 815-1829

Information we collect

When you request a quote or contact us, we collect the information you give us: your name, email address, phone number (optional), project details (products, quantities, sizes, colours, print locations, desired date) and files you send us, such as your logo or artwork.

We do not collect information for advertising purposes and we do not sell your information.

Why we use it

  • To respond to your request and prepare your quote;
  • To prepare your mockups and produce, deliver and invoice your order;
  • To communicate with you about your project.

We use your information only for these purposes, unless you consent to another use or the law requires it.

Quote form and security

To protect the form from spam and bots, we use Cloudflare Turnstile, a security check service from Cloudflare, Inc. It analyzes technical signals from your browser (for example your IP address and device characteristics) only to confirm that a real person is using the form.

Depending on how the form is configured, your request reaches us either through your own email app, or through a secure service hosted by Cloudflare that forwards it to us by email using an email delivery provider.

Cookies and similar technologies

Our site uses no advertising or tracking cookies. It only uses what it needs to work: storing your cookie choice in your browser, and the Cloudflare security check described above. If we ever add an analytics tool, it will be off by default and only turned on with your consent. You can change your choice at any time using the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Who has access to your information

Only the people at All Things Printed who need it to handle your request or order have access to it. We also use service providers to host the site, secure the form and deliver emails. These providers may process information outside Quebec, including in the United States. We share only what is necessary and make sure your information is adequately protected.

Retention

We keep your information as long as needed to respond to your request, complete your order and meet our legal obligations (for example, tax requirements), and then destroy it securely.

Your rights

You may at any time request access to your personal information, its correction or deletion, or withdraw your consent, by writing to info@allthingsprinted.ca. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date of the last update appears at the top of the page.
