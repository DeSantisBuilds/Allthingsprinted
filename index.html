What we print

  • Custom DTF apparel printing

    Direct-to-film (DTF) printing transfers your full-colour design onto garments. Bring us your artwork, logo, or idea for shirts, hoodies, hats and more.

  • Vinyl lettering & graphics

    Cut vinyl lettering and graphics for names, logos, and custom designs. Let us know what you want lettered and where it’s going.
